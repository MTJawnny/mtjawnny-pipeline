## 2. The lead case proves the mechanism, and needs no ratification at all

**`rule:create-token-clue` does not exist — in any status.** Never authored,
never killed, never merged.

- `clue` is **already ratified token-type vocabulary** (§8 rule 4).
- `create-token-<type>` is an **already ratified grammar family** (batch-5 D14).
- Batch-5 D14 **names it explicitly** as a future sibling: *"Future siblings:
  create-token-food, create-token-blood, create-token-lander,
  **create-token-clue**, etc."*
- Grammar §11: *"A virtual node instantiates the moment one quote-verified
  member arrives — no fresh ratification (the grammar was ratified)."*
- **132 gate-passing cards print "investigate", which CR 701.16 defines as
  "create a Clue token".**

So the node should have self-instantiated a year of batches ago. It did not,
because nothing was looking. That is the whole step-3 thesis in one axis.

**Correction to a document CDR-PROPOSALS rests on:** F-E cites

## 3. Why this is not "author 40 axes"

Per §6b every distinct printed shape earns an axis. Measured delivery shapes
per action:

| action | cards | distinct delivery shapes |
|---|--:|--:|
| investigate | 132 | **15** |
| goad | 56 | 11 |
| manifest | 64 | 11 |
| amass | 57 | 9 |
| venture into the dungeon | 46 | 8 |

Investigate alone would be ~15 axes. Across 40 actions, hundreds. Authoring
those up front would be exactly the empty-hypothesis bloat
`CORPUS-PASS-PLAN` §2 forbids: *"an axis with zero members is a hypothesis, not
a ratified rule."*

**The ratified mechanism already handles this.** Ratify the *grammar*, not the
axes; nodes instantiate on first quote-verified member. The dominant shapes
(spell-unmarked, `etb-`, `activated-`, `attack-trigger-`) carry most of the
membership and will instantiate immediately; the long tail arrives at the
corpus pass or never.

## 4. THE CALL — ratify CR 701 action names as slug vocabulary

One batch ratification, the same shape as Q5's ~30-token batch.

**Proposed:** the CR 701 keyword-action names enter the closed EFFECT
vocabulary as slug tokens, each anchored to its rule number, and the grammar
family `[DELIVERY]-<cr-action>` is ratified so nodes self-instantiate per §11.

Why one batch rather than 40 rulings: these are not coinages. They are CR terms
of art with rule numbers, which §6a already makes binding — *"templating words
are CR terms of art... hardcoded to the mechanic they name."* Ratifying them
individually would be re-deciding what the CR already decided.

**What this does NOT cover, and I am flagging rather than assuming:**

- Some actions have **no ratified object vocabulary** for what they produce.
  `incubate` makes an Incubator token; `amass` makes an Army; `manifest` makes
  a face-down 2/2. Those token types are **not** in §8 rule 4's list, so those
  specific slugs still need vocabulary. `investigate`/`clue` is the lucky case
  where everything already exists.
- **`activate` (1,461 cards) is not an effect** — it is CR 701.2, the act of
  using an activated ability, already carried by the `activated-` DELIVERY
  slot. It should be **excluded** from this batch. Including it would create a
  slug token that duplicates a delivery marker.
- **Un-set and mechanically-narrow actions** (`earthbend` 37, `waterbend` 29,
  `airbend`, `open an attraction` 20) are legal-somewhere and pass Gate #0, but
  are set-local. Worth ratifying with the rest, or deferring — your call.

**DEFAULT: ratify the batch, excluding `activate`; instantiate
`rule:create-token-clue` immediately under existing vocabulary (no ratification
needed); bring the token-type gaps (Incubator, Army, face-down) as a separate
vocabulary question.**
