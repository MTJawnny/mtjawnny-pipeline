# ABILITY NAMES ARE FLAVOR WORDS, AND A MODE IS NOT AN ABILITY
## CR 700.2 / CR 207.2d — 2026-08-06

Captain, 2026-08-06, on **Black Market Connections**:

> *"Some cards have ability names. `Sell Contraband —`, `Buy Information —`,
> `Hire a Mercenary —` are just ability names, they currently have no effective
> gameplay significance. They can be essentially ignored. Read CR on this. They
> are fundamentally different than abilities like landfall."*

**The CR agrees, and states the difference in one sentence.** Acting on it
closed **22** gap lines and stopped **1** wrong ratified token.

---

## 1. The CR draws exactly this line

> **CR 207.2d** — *"Similar to ability words, **flavor words** appear in italics
> at the beginning of some abilities. Flavor words provide a flavorful
> description of abilities, but they have **no special rules meaning and are not
> listed in the Comprehensive Rules**. While an **ability word** ties together
> several abilities with **similar functionality**, each **flavor word** is
> **tailored to the specific ability it appears with**."*

| | `Landfall` | `Sell Contraband` |
|---|---|---|
| CR rule | **207.2c** | **207.2d** |
| listed in the CR | **yes**, in a closed list of 61 | **no**, explicitly not |
| scope | ties several cards together | one card, one ability |
| corpus lines | **174** | **1** |
| is it vocabulary? | **yes** | **never** |

This is the same cut the 2026-08-06 ability-word pass already implements
(`98af57a`): CR 207.2c is **parsed** as a closed list, and everything else that
shape-matches is a CR 207.2d flavor word to be stripped and discarded.

**Implication for the thesaurus, stated plainly:** a flavor word must never
become an axis, a token, or a tag. It is a card-unique string; ratifying one
would create a 1-member axis that means nothing and can never gain a second
member. `Sell Contraband` is not `landfall` and never will be.

## 2. What was actually broken — the BULLET

The flavor words Captain named sit on **modal bullets**, and `_DASH_PREFIX`
tested from the start of the line. `•` is not in the flavor-word character
class, so **every mode name was refused** and the mode's ability text never
reached its branch.

CR 700.2 settles what the bullet is:

> *"A spell or ability is modal if it has two or more options in a **bulleted
> list** preceded by instructions for a player to choose a number of those
> options, such as 'Choose one —.' **Each of those options is a mode.**"*

The bullet is **list punctuation**. The mode's ability text begins after it. So
the prefix test now skips it: `^\s*(?:•\s*)?([^—]{1,40}?)\s*—\s*`.

### Measured

| | |
|---|--:|
| bulleted lines carrying an em-dash prefix | **182** |
| ...delivery **inherited** from a detected modal header (D3) | 57 |
| ...parsed on their **own** | 125 |
| ...of those, **unrouted** | 124 |
| **gap lines closed by the fix** | **22** |

Every one of the 22 is a **Siege-cycle-shaped** mode — a mode that is a genuine
triggered ability *of the permanent*, chosen once as it enters:

```
• Khans   — At the beginning of your draw step, draw an additional card, …   draw-step-trigger
• Abzan   — At the beginning of your end step, put a +1/+1 counter on …      end-step-trigger
• Temur   — Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery spell, …                 cast-trigger
• Dragons — Whenever a creature you control leaves the battlefield, …        any-leaves-battlefield-trigger
• Legion  — Whenever a creature you control dies, …                          any-death-trigger
```

Zero re-routes, zero lines appeared or vanished, row count unchanged,
`keyword_homes` unchanged, determinism ×2 byte-identical, invariance 1 (the
known artifact), conservation audit clean (2,306 → 2,483 prefixes stripped, 0
violations).

**Black Market Connections itself does not move** — its header
(`At the beginning of your first main phase, choose one or more —`) *is*
detected, so all three modes already inherited `precombat-main-phase-trigger`
via D3. The fix now also identifies `Sell Contraband` as the flavor word it is,
which matters for everything downstream of the strip, not for this card's
routing.

## 3. THE FINDING THIS SURFACED — better recall handed out a WRONG token

The first run closed **23** lines, not 22. The extra one was wrong:

```
Hawkeye, Master Marksman
  Trick Arrows — Whenever Hawkeye becomes tapped, you may pay {1} up to three
                 times. When you do, choose up to that many.
  • Net       — Target creature can't block this turn.
  • Explosive — Hawkeye deals 2 damage to target player.      ← None -> `static`
  • Boomerang — Discard a card, then draw a card.
```

Once `Explosive` was stripped, the line read `~ deals 2 damage to target
player` and the **CR 113.3a closure** claimed it: *a spell ability exists only
on an instant or sorcery, so a card with no instant/sorcery face leaves CR
113.3's four-category enumeration closed on `static`.*

**That closure is valid for a printed ability and does not reach a mode.**
CR 700.2 says each option **is a mode** — an option *inside* an ability, not one
of CR 113.3's four categories. Hawkeye's modes belong to a **CR 603.12 reflexive
trigger** (*"When you do, choose up to that many"*), and a created ability's
delivery belongs to its creator (§2d).

So the branch now refuses a bulleted line, and `• Explosive` returns to being an
honest gap until its header is detected.

**This is the standing lesson one layer up.** *"A fallback is a wrong answer
with a ratified name"* was about a `deals? damage to` arm defaulting its
recipient. Here **improving recall** did it: a line that had been correctly
reported as unknown became confidently wrong. A gap-closing diff that scores
`None → ratified` as pure profit cannot see this — **every closed gap still has
to be read**, which is why the procedure says so.

## 4. Why the remaining 102 stay unrouted, and whose call that is

`• Net —` and `• Boomerang —` are still unrouted, as are ~102 other mode lines.
Their headers are not detected, because `_MODAL_HEADER_RE` requires the header
to **end** in an em-dash and Hawkeye's ends in *"choose up to that many."*

CR 700.2 defines modality by **the bulleted list plus a choose instruction**,
not by punctuation — so the regex is narrower than the rule. That is
`PUNCTUATION-RESCAN-2026-08-06.md` §4, re-measured at **45 certain / 102
candidate headers, 259 bullets**, and it remains **Captain's call**:
`_MODAL_HEADER_RE` is part of the ratified DET preprocessing standard v1 and is
shared with `det_scan_texts()`. Nothing here touched it.

Fixing it would let all ~102 inherit their header's delivery, which is the
correct answer for every one of them — including `• Explosive`.
