## 1. Fixed this pass — 9 evidence corrections

### 1a. Three were defects introduced EARLIER THE SAME DAY

The multi-axis `adds` in the membership packet carried each card's original
assertion verbatim, on my stated assumption that "the same quote that proves
the ability proves every axis that ability satisfies." **That is false when the
original quote is a fragment scoped to its source axis.**

| axis | quote as landed | proves the axis? |
|---|---|---|
| `combat-damage-to-player-forced-owner-bounce` | "That player discards a card." | **No — that is the OTHER mode** |
| `permanent-control-theft` | "…you may sacrifice it." | **No — never reaches the theft** |
| `combat-damage-to-player-permanent-control-theft` | "…you may sacrifice it." | **No — truncated before the effect** |

Caught within one tier of the re-audit that surfaced it. The mechanism is now
fixed as well as the data: `foundry_membership_move.py` **halts** on any `add`
that does not state the quote proving its own destination axis.

## 2. Open findings — need rulings

### 2a. `rule:cast-trigger-tutor-to-battlefield` / Garruk, Caller of Beasts — MISFILE

Cited quote: *"Whenever you cast a creature spell, you may search your library
for a creature card, put it onto the battlefield, then shuffle."*

That text is **the emblem's**, granted by Garruk's −7. The card itself has no
cast-trigger; it is a planeswalker with three loyalty abilities. Under §2,
delivery is `loyalty`.

**This is the third instance of one class**, and that is the real finding:

| card | axis | actual source of the ability |
|---|---|---|
| Garruk, Caller of Beasts | `cast-trigger-tutor-to-battlefield` | an **emblem** from a −7 |
| Jace, Cunning Castaway | `combat-damage-to-player-loot` | a **delayed trigger** from a +1 |
| The Wandering Emperor, Kaya, Nahiri | `etb-*` axes | **loyalty abilities** |

**Proposed rule for ratification:** an ability that a card *creates* — via an
emblem, a delayed trigger, a granted ability, or a token's text — is not
delivered by the card that creates it. The delivery belongs to the creating
ability (`loyalty`, `delayed`, …). Membership on the effect axis may still be
right; the delivery axis is not. This governs at minimum the 39 ETB findings
plus these two.

### 2b. `rule:leaves-battlefield-trigger-create-token-food` / Brandywine Farmer — INCOMPLETE

*"When this creature enters **or** leaves the battlefield, create a Food token."*

The card is **both** an ETB and an LTB trigger, and holds membership only on
the LTB axis. Under the multi-axis rule ratified this session it also belongs
on an `etb-create-token-food` axis, **which does not exist**.

Grammar §11 already names this exact card as the case that should have
self-instantiated its grammar family: *"the b7 Brandywine Farmer case is the
model: `leaves-battlefield-create-token-food` should have self-instantiated."*
So this is a known gap that the multi-axis ruling now makes actionable.

**Needs:** ratify `rule:etb-create-token-food` and add Brandywine Farmer.

### 2c. `rule:targets-highest-life-opponent` — ZERO MEMBERS, no documented intent

Four axes have zero members. Three are Captain-registered placeholders whose
definitions say so explicitly (*"no hand-picked examples yet, membership filled
by derivation at the full-corpus pass"*) — `energy-outlet-infinite`,
`gives-energy-counters-condition`, `targeted-battle-damage`. Those are fine.

`rule:targets-highest-life-opponent` is **`source: B-only`** with no such note.
A model-proposed axis with zero members has never been evidenced by anything.
**Needs:** kill it, or seed it at the corpus pass — but it should not sit
active and unevidenced.
