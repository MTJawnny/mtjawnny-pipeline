## 1. The shape

A line whose **first words** are *"As long as"* states a condition under which
something is true. That is CR 113.3d's definition:

> **CR 113.3d** — *"Static abilities are written as statements. **They're simply
> true.**"*

CR 604.2 supplies the duration, and it is the rule that decides the hard cases:

> **CR 604.2** — *"Static abilities create continuous effects… These effects are
> active as long as the permanent with the ability remains on the battlefield
> and has the ability, **or as long as the object with the ability remains in
> the appropriate zone**, as described in rule 113.6."*

So a static that functions from the **graveyard** (Anger, Brawn, Filth, Valor,
Wonder), from the **library** (Chittering Illuminator, Pearl Lake Warden) or
from the **stack** (Kaervek's Torch) is still `static`. **The branch therefore
takes no permanent-side type gate.** The two instant/sorcery lines it claims are
the Torrent of Lava case that STEP-2A §3b already ruled correct: §1's unmarked
default governs a spell's **resolution effect**, not a static ability that
functions in another zone.

## 2. LINE-INITIAL is the whole claim, and it does two jobs

### 2a. It excludes CR 611.2b, which is a spell's DURATION and not a static

> **CR 611.2b** — *"Some continuous effects generated by the **resolution of a
> spell or ability** have durations worded **'for as long as . . . .'**"*
> Example given: Master Thief — *"gain control of target artifact **for as long
> as** you control this creature."*

`^as long as` excludes the `for` form by construction. **Measured: ZERO lines in
the corpus open with "for as long as"**, so the exclusion costs nothing today —
but the shape it guards is real and printed on hundreds of cards mid-line, and a
`\bas long as\b` test would have swept every one of them in.

### 2b. Mid-line "as long as" belongs to whatever owns the line

A condition attached to something else's effect does not make that thing a
static. Reaching this branch only at the **TAIL** is the other half of the
guard: loyalty, activated, every trigger family and `replacement` have all
already declined the line before it is tested. A new tail branch can only claim
lines that already reached `spell-or-static`, which is why the diff shows
**zero** re-routes — the safest possible placement, and the one
`PRE-STEP-2-AUDIT` §6 exists to insist on.

## 3. All 443 were read. Two shapes were checked against ratified law.

Reading found **no** trigger word, **no** activation cost and **no** CR 614.1a–c
template outside a quoted created ability, in any of the 443.

### 3a. 7 lines create a PREVENTION effect — and prevention is not §2e

Thunderstaff, Sanwell, Multiclass Baldric, Bronze Horse, Spirit of Resistance,
Woolly Razorback, Camel. **§2e** (a static ability that *generates* a
replacement effect takes `replacement`) does **not** reach them, because the CR
makes prevention a **parallel** category, not a subset:

> **CR 615.1** — *"Some continuous effects are prevention effects. **Like
> replacement effects (see rule 614)**, prevention effects apply continuously as
> events happen…"*

§2's `replacement` row is anchored to **614.1a–c** only, and §2e's ratified scope
is explicitly *"where the same sub-rule's templated text is a CR 614.1a–c
replacement template."* Prevention is CR 615.1a. **Ratified practice already
agrees:** Armament of Nyx carries a prevention effect on ratified `static` today,
via the aura branch. Routing these to `static` is consistent with that, not a
fresh decision.

### 3b. Cloud, Midgar Mercenary is CR 603.2d, not CR 614

*"…if a triggered ability of Cloud or an Equipment attached to it triggers, that
ability **triggers an additional time**."* The CR files this under **triggered
abilities**, not under replacement effects:

> **CR 603.2d** — *"An ability may state that a triggered ability triggers
> additional times."*

No 614.1a–c template, so `static` stands.

## 4. THE MEASUREMENT WAS WRONG BY 43 — Gate 4, and the check was mine

**My probe measured 400. The classifier moved 443.** The gap is not data, it is
a boundary my probe drew and the classifier does not:

| | |
|---|--:|
| probe matched `^as long as` on the **raw** line | 400 |
| classifier matches on `body`, **after `ABILITY_WORD.sub("", raw)`** | **443** |

The 43 are ability-word-prefixed statics — **Threshold** (18), **Corrupted**,
**Metalcraft**, **Delirium**, **Lieutenant**, **Fateful hour**, **Coven**,
**Celebration**, **Eminence**, plus four card-specific words (Cosmic Awareness,
Negative, Prismatic Gallery, Dynastic Command Node). **CR 207.2c**: an ability
word *"has no rules meaning"*, so stripping it and reading the statement beneath
is correct, and every one of the 43 is a static.

All 43 were then read individually. One is spell-side and correct: **Viral
Spawning** (Sorcery) — *"Corrupted — As long as an opponent has three or more
poison counters **and this card is in your graveyard**, it has flashback…"* —
CR 604.2's "appropriate zone" clause exactly.

**The lesson is the standing one at a new address.** Gate 4 says suspect the
check; this is the first instance where the disagreement was between a
*measurement probe* and the *classifier*, rather than between a checker and a
ratified list. A probe that preprocesses differently from the thing it is
measuring will under-report, and it under-reports **silently** — the 43 would
have landed as an unexplained surplus in the diff, not as an error. **A probe
must consume the same preprocessing as the classifier**, which is the
`--orphans` finding of Gate 3b (4 consumers use `det_scan_texts()`, 19 bypass
it) arriving one layer down.

## 5. Side effect, reported: KEYWORD_HOME 148 → 150, moving ZERO lines

`build_keyword_homes` derives a keyword's home by running `parse_delivery` over
its **CR templated text**, so a new branch can change the map. Five CR 702
keywords state their text as a line-initial *"As long as"*:

| CR | keyword | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 702.61 | Split Second | `static` (class fallback) | `static` — unchanged |
| 702.178 | Max Speed | `static` (class fallback) | `static` — unchanged |
| 702.187 | Mayhem | `static` (class fallback) | `static` — unchanged |
| **702.169** | **Solved** | **absent** | **`static`** |
| **702.186** | **∞ (Infinity)** | **absent** | **`static`** |

Solved and ∞ were absent because `effective_classes` returns `[]` for them — the
CR does not state their class in the parsed form — so the existing fallback could
not fire. **Zero corpus lines moved through the keyword path** (the diff's 443
are all `static-condition`), and **zero cards print a bare `Solved` or `∞` line**.

**The ability-parameter trap does not widen here.** All three of Solved, ∞ and
Max Speed have a parameter that is a whole ABILITY (*"As long as this Case is
solved, **[ability text]**"*), and `build_keyword_forms` already **refuses** any
such form — the guard added when `Max speed — [Ability]` was caught overwriting
Pride of the Road's `begin-combat-trigger`. The refusal is on the FORM matcher,
which is the reachable path; `KEYWORD_HOME` alone is reachable only by a bare
keyword line, of which there are none. Reported rather than suppressed, because
a latent widening that moves zero lines today is exactly the shape of "a
ratified token with no emitter".
