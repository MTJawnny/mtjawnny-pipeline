## Purpose

This addendum closes Objective 5 and records the Captain's post-research architectural clarification without changing the underlying mechanical research evidence.

Objective 5 answered the architectural question: a true gameplay-function thesaurus cannot be obtained by merely tuning S9's shared-ACTIVE-axis ranking. A downstream functional layer is required above Oracle/CR-grounded mechanical facts, with multi-parent functional membership, context handling, provenance, hard negatives, independent candidate-discovery/order/explanation evaluation, and a precommitted holdout.

No S16B implementation is authorized by this closure.

## Closure rulings

### 1. Community vocabulary is discovery evidence, not final semantic authority

The Objective 5 research used familiar community terms such as `ramp`, `cantrip`, `value`, `stax`, `protection`, and others to discover useful functional neighborhoods and disagreements.

Those disagreements are **research input**, not a permanent requirement that Foundry expose an ambiguous vocabulary.

The project direction is now:

> Community vocabulary supplies names, examples, boundary disputes, and candidate distinctions. Foundry owns the final definitions.

A term that becomes a searchable/filterable Foundry label must eventually have a versioned, deterministic project definition precise enough that identical mechanical/context facts produce the same membership decision. Foundry may narrow, split, rename, or replace a familiar community term when necessary to remove ambiguity.

This does **not** allow a derived Foundry vocabulary definition to overwrite Oracle or Comprehensive Rules mechanical truth. Mechanical facts remain the lower authority substrate; functional labels are derived above them.

### 2. Vocabulary-dependent Objective 5 labels remain provisional

The card relationships, source evidence, positive examples, hard negatives, and context examples gathered in Objective 5 remain valid preflight evidence.

However, labels whose meaning depends on unresolved vocabulary boundaries — for example `ramp`, `cantrip`, `card advantage`, `value engine`, `stax`, or similar terms — are **PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_LABELS_NOT_PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY** until the vocabulary work is ratified.

The relation evidence must not be discarded merely because a label later changes. Example pairs may survive while their parent term, child term, threshold, or explanation schema is refined.

No S9/S16B output may be used to relabel the benchmark into agreement with the implementation.

### 3. Multi-label membership is an explicit architectural invariant

Foundry functional vocabulary is not a one-card/one-role taxonomy.

A card, mode, ability, or relevant occurrence may satisfy multiple functional parents simultaneously when the underlying facts justify them. No `primary_role` assumption is permitted merely for storage or ranking convenience.

Examples discussed during closure include cards that may simultaneously participate in low-cost card-access, deep-selection, card-advantage, modality, graveyard-resource, or other functional families. The lower mechanical coordinates must remain available to explain both shared jobs and meaningful differences.

### 4. Objective 6 owns vocabulary normalization and ratification

The broad community-lexicon inventory and exact Foundry definitions are now a separate project objective:

**Objective 6 — Foundry Functional Vocabulary Specification**

Objective 6 is responsible for turning useful familiar vocabulary into precise Foundry contracts, including at minimum:

- canonical Foundry name and user-facing aliases;
- exact definition;
- parent/child relationships;
- required and disqualifying predicates;
- numeric, timing, cost, zone, and resource-accounting thresholds where relevant;
- context class requirements;
- multi-label interaction;
- gold positives;
- hard negatives / near misses;
- edge cases;
- Oracle/CR facts used;
- provenance for community terminology considered;
- versioned Captain rulings.

The working community lexicon created after Objective 5 is **Objective 6 working material**, not an Objective 5 semantic artifact and not yet repository authority.

### 5. S16B semantic freeze has two prerequisites

Before S16B semantics can be frozen for implementation, both of the following must exist and be accepted:

1. **S16A lossless mechanical/semantic substrate**, preserving the relevant Oracle/CR-derived facts needed for functional derivation; and
2. **Objective 6 ratified Foundry functional vocabulary**, including the contracts needed by the frozen benchmark and explanation schema.

The prior Objective 5 holdout law remains: labels, context classes, coordinate schema, benchmark/open set, negative families, ordering metrics, explanation requirements, provenance requirements, source cutoff, pinned corpus/CR, and blind-holdout commitment must be frozen before implementation tuning can contaminate them.
