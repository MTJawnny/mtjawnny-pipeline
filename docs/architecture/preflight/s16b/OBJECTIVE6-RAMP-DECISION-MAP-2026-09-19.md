# Objective 6 — Ramp Decision Map — Audit Revision

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** **RAMP FAMILY RETAINED; MECHANISMS DEMOTED TO SIGNATURES/COORDINATES; FAST MANA DEMOTED TO SURFACED COMMUNITY TAG**

## 1. Ramp survives the whole-vocabulary audit

**Ramp remains a strong player-facing functional family.**

Working functional meaning:

> **Ramp** — a card/effect increases the controller's usable mana or mana-producing capacity beyond ordinary resource development.

The family is justified because:

- players search/count the function directly;
- the outcome remains coherent across permanent and temporary mechanisms;
- Searcher B benefits from grouping functionally adjacent acceleration while preserving how it is achieved;
- the major edge cases can be represented by harder coordinates rather than exceptions to membership.

## 2. What changes

The earlier map risked turning every useful mana mechanism into a named child and tied several examples to the now-demoted canonical `Engine` tree.

The revised structure is shallower:

```text
Ramp [surfaced functional family]
|
+-- hard mechanism/event/resource facts
    +-- independent mana source
    +-- additional land play permission
    +-- mana source creation
    +-- mana ability/source granting
    +-- fixed source-output augmentation
    +-- proportional mana multiplication
    +-- nonmana-resource -> mana conversion
    +-- event -> mana / mana-capable-object conversion
    +-- state-scaled mana output
    +-- mana-source reuse
    +-- burst / one-shot mana
```

These mechanism labels are explanatory views over event/resource coordinates. They do not all need canonical child-family status.

## 3. Dependency facts

Conditionality remains decomposed mechanically rather than promoted to `Conditional Ramp`.

Preserve facts such as:

- extra land inventory required;
- productive host required;
- source/host reuse dependency;
- opponent/game event required;
- life/card/creature/object consumed;
- enters tapped or delayed;
- spending restriction;
- output scaling basis;
- fixed vs proportional augmentation;
- direct vs mediated conversion;
- persistence/consumption of the created source.

## 4. Fast Mana revision

`Fast Mana` remains valuable player/community vocabulary, but the audit rejects its previous status as a rigid canonical child based on the `initial relevant use` threshold.

Treasonous Ogre exposes the arbitrariness: repeated same-turn activations can cross deployment break-even even though the first activation does not.

Canonical Foundry should store measurable acceleration facts instead:

- deployment mana cost;
- immediate mana output;
- same-turn net mana after first relevant use;
- activations required for break-even / positive mana delta where finite;
- nonmana resource costs;
- summoning-sickness/timing constraints;
- persistence.

**Fast Mana is therefore a surfaced community-derived tag/projection over these facts, not a hard child required by the canonical ontology.**

Conventional positives such as Dark Ritual, Sol Ring, Lotus Petal, Elvish Spirit Guide, and Mana Vault remain easy to surface. Boundary cards can be explained rather than forced through an arbitrary binary law.

## 5. Ritual / burst mana

One-shot/burst mana is a hard mechanism pattern.

`Ritual` remains a useful community/search alias over appropriate burst-mana effects. It is not synonymous with Ramp or Fast Mana.

## 6. Ramp vs cost/payment mechanics

Keep the following distinct:

- **Ramp:** increases mana/capacity;
- **Cost Reduction:** makes qualifying total costs smaller;
- **Alternative Cost:** formal CR cost paid instead of mana cost;
- **Payment Method:** changes how an already-determined cost/symbol can be paid;
- **Direct Placement:** places an object without casting that object.

Strategic adjacency does not erase the rules path.

## 7. Ramp vs processor/repeatability facts

The canonical `Mana Engine` child is retired with the Engine hierarchy.

Cards such as Lotus Cobra, Treasonous Ogre, Skirge Familiar, and mana-doubling/reuse systems still preserve processor facts:

- input event/resource;
- firing cap;
- repeatability;
- output amount/type;
- processor retention;
- external fuel;
- opportunity window.

UI/search may still use `mana engine` as a derived/community handle over `Ramp + processor facts`.

## 8. Mana preservation

Effects that preserve already-produced mana across steps/phases are not automatically Ramp because they may change persistence without increasing production/capacity.

Keep mana persistence as a resource-state coordinate.

## 9. Role-compression consequence

Cards that satisfy several functional families still contribute to each underlying function. No special Ramp subclass is needed.

The later derived multi-function/role-compression view can report that one card contributes Ramp plus other functions.

## 10. Control boundary

No S16B freeze, implementation acceptance, broad corpus reclassification, merge, accepted-head/main movement, AQ4 resumption, Bridge activation, or Step6 is authorized.
