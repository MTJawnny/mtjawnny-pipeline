## Key post-audit corrections retained by the closed snapshot

### Engine

Engine/Card Engine/Mana Engine are derived/search/community language over hard processor facts, not a canonical parent hierarchy.

### Card Filtering / Sample Selection

Generic Card Filtering is a broad UI/search umbrella and low-information similarity signal. **Sample Selection** is the independently queryable finite-library-sample pattern retained by S16B.

### Card Resource Delta

Card Resource Delta is a derived accounting fact over distinct underlying card-origin resources/access, not a semantic family. Stateful closeout fixtures later added explicit observation-window, identity-turnover, pending-acquisition, copy, repeated-execution, and multiplayer discipline.

### Card Access Differential

Retired. No replacement metric was made canonical.

### Payment semantics

The old Alternate Payment umbrella was retired. Cost Reduction, Alternative Cost, Additional Cost, and Payment Method remain distinct.

### Direct Placement

The cast-versus-put distinction survives. Direct Placement records movement/placement without the ordinary cast/play deployment action.

### Top-Library Access

Positive membership requires actual use permission. Tutor-to-top, reveal-only, inspection-only, and reorder-only effects are not Top-Library Access merely because they mention the top of a library.
