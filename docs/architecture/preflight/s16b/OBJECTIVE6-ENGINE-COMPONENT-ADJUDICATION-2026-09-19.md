# Objective 6 — Engine-Component Adjudication — Historical Finding / Revised Placement

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** **MECHANICAL FINDINGS RETAINED; CANONICAL ENGINE MEMBERSHIP SUPERSEDED BY PROCESSOR-FACT MODEL**

## 1. What survives

The Nest of Scarabs adversarial case exposed a durable distinction that remains important after the whole-vocabulary audit:

1. **trigger/firing multiplicity**;
2. **output magnitude**;
3. **repeatability / processor recurrence**;
4. **feedback supplied by an external multi-card assembly**.

Nest of Scarabs can compress several counters into one trigger while still making `that many` tokens. That proves a `one or more` trigger is not enough to infer either low output or lack of scalable magnitude.

The card's output scaling and its participation in loops are separate facts.

## 2. What changes

The earlier version of this record adjudicated `Engine: NO` under a canonical Engine family.

The later whole-vocabulary audit demotes Engine from canonical family membership to a **derived/search/community label over hard processor facts**.

Therefore the durable canonical record for Nest of Scarabs should be approximately:

- listener/event: one-or-more -1/-1 counters placed on a creature;
- trigger aggregation: one trigger for the qualifying counter-placement event;
- output: creature-token creation;
- output magnitude: number of counters in the event;
- feedback/recurrence: not supplied by this text alone;
- loop participation: relationship-layer fact when other cards feed outputs back into inputs.

Whether a player-facing search calls this an `engine component` is downstream terminology, not canonical card truth.

## 3. General rule retained

> **Scalable output magnitude, repeated processor firing, and assembled-loop participation are different mechanical properties.**

Do not infer one from another.

This remains a required adversarial rule for corpus analysis.

## 4. Infinite combos remain separate

`can go infinite`, `is part of an infinite combo`, and `forms a deterministic win line` remain relationship/strategy facts.

They are not primitive evidence for repeatability or processor structure on the individual card.
