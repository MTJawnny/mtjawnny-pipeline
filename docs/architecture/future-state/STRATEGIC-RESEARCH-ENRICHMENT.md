# Future State — Strategic Research Enrichment

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** **CAPTAIN-DIRECTED FUTURE STRATEGIC LAYER — TABLED; NO CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZATION**

## 1. Purpose

Foundry's canonical semantic substrate is Oracle/rules-derived mechanical truth.

A later strategic layer may enrich that substrate with community/deck-construction knowledge, prior-art observations, and behavioral recommendation evidence.

This material must remain visibly separate from canonical card mechanics.

Conceptual layering:

```text
Layer 1: Foundry canonical semantic substrate
Layer 2: externally sourced strategic/research enrichment
Layer 3: Commander/deck strategic reasoning
Layer 4: probabilistic deck-context reasoning
Layer 5: explainable recommendations / Complete My Deck
```

No higher layer may silently rewrite Layer 1.

---

## 2. Research provenance requirement

External strategy claims should be stored with claim-level provenance, including where practical:

- source;
- publication/access date;
- claim text or normalized claim;
- source type;
- reliability/confidence assessment;
- supporting/conflicting sources;
- relevant format/metagame/time context;
- distinction between observation, opinion, measured behavior, and canonical rules fact.

Conflicts should remain visible rather than resolved by silently selecting whichever source supports a preferred narrative.

---

## 3. CommanderSalt prior art

CommanderSalt is a primary strategic-system prior-art subject.

Future analysis should study, insofar as public/documented behavior permits:

- weighting choices;
- distinctions between card/deck functions;
- interaction models;
- composition heuristics;
- relationship structures;
- nonlinear effects;
- failure modes;
- strategic observations encoded by the product.

Foundry should **not** conform to or copy CommanderSalt's deck-lethality/four-lane architecture simply because it exists.

The purpose is to learn from the prior art while preserving Foundry's independent semantic architecture.

---

## 4. Recommander prior art

Recommander is a primary behavioral/recommendation comparison source.

Published behavior indicates a public API that accepts commander context, optional partner, and current deck contents and returns ranked recommendations/recommendation scores based on aggregated public Commander decklists/trends.

Future use should treat this as behavioral/recommendation prior art rather than canonical mechanical truth.

Constraints:

- use documented public interfaces/behavior;
- respect applicable terms;
- do not scrape the site in violation of those constraints;
- do not attempt to reverse engineer proprietary model internals;
- use controlled input changes to study observable recommendation behavior where appropriate.

Useful future questions include:

- how recommendations change as deck contents change;
- whether recommendations primarily reflect popularity, synergy, archetype co-occurrence, commander identity, or other observable patterns;
- where behavior differs from Foundry's mechanically derived semantic similarity;
- where Foundry's Budget Swapper / Complete My Deck capabilities can add information not represented by co-occurrence alone.

---

## 5. Broader strategic knowledge

Potential future strategic enrichment includes:

- archetype conventions (stax-like strategic patterns may be discussed here even where `Stax` is retired from canonical semantic vocabulary);
- aggro/midrange/control/combo strategic framing;
- common deck-role expectations;
- win-condition identification;
- combo popularity/prevalence;
- political value in multiplayer;
- interaction density/conventions;
- finishers;
- underrated-card discussions;
- common commander-specific packages;
- metagame/format conventions.

These are contextual claims whose truth can change with time and population.

They therefore require provenance and should not be encoded as eternal card-level mechanical truth.

---

## 6. Product differentiation to preserve

Foundry should not be positioned primarily as another generic recommendation/co-occurrence engine.

The intended base differentiation remains:

1. **MTG Thesaurus** — mechanically grounded semantic/referral layer;
2. **Budget mode / Budget Swapper workflow** — substitutions under price constraints while preserving/exposing semantic differences;
3. **Deck Workspace / semantic deltas** — factual composition and replacement changes;
4. **Complete My Deck / Deck Finisher** — later strategic recommendations built on canonical semantics plus contextual reasoning.

A future Budget Swapper may quantify expected functional/power loss under substitution, but any such strategic/power model must remain separate from the canonical semantic identity of the cards.

---

## 7. Research is not semantic authority

External sources may suggest concepts worth checking in the canonical semantic layer.

Example:

- if community sources repeatedly group a set of cards under a concept, Foundry may investigate whether that concept cleanly distills into mechanical primitives/predicates/semantic DNA;
- the external popularity of the label does not itself authorize the category.

The Objective 6 governing heuristic still applies:

> **Prefer easy, faithful distillation over preserving terminology. If a concept fights the substrate, make the concept prove that it deserves additional structure.**
