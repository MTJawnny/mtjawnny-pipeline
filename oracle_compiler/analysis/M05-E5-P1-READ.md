## Reading notes

- 109 rows compare names: "with the same name as" a target, a spell, a chosen, revealed, discarded or exiled card, a permanent, or another object. Under CR 201.2a the clause asks whether two objects share a name. It does not name a single object, so recording no endpoint is right. The object on the right side of "as" ("that creature", "the exiled card") may itself be a reference, but it is a separate span that other rules handle. It is not this row's endpoint.
- 9 rows compare mana value (CR 202.3) and one compares "power and/or same toughness" (CR 208.1). They are the same printed "the same ... as" shape.
- Reflexive comparisons ("as one another", "as it", "as another card in their hand") are still comparisons among objects in a set, not a reference to one object.
- Conditions ("if it has", "unless it has", "that doesn't have") and counts ("the number of cards ... with") use the comparison as a filter or a test. That does not change what the phrase means.
